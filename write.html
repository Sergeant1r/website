2Bike

My chain runs dry

My brakes squeal and cry

My skinny wheels flat

My bag on my back heavy

Theres a stop sign but my mind wont stop moving

The gears keep cranking

Wheels keep spinning

Pedals keep pushing

And the spring loaded pawls click

They hum, a sound of mechanical symbiosis

That keeps moving

/

I start riding my bike now,

And the ribbons start dancing to the song

And the trees sway freely

That's when the synth of the world plays

It seeps into my ears and sets my mind a haze

We glide weightlessly in the ecstasy of absence

Finally, my bag feels lighter

And I think I can ride through the sun's fury

//

Dandelions

Before child and baby and infant, they swim

Eager to win life

And I can't stop picking my scabs

The painful pleasure of human regeneration

The condemned miracle of the human mind

/

The fluffy white seeds of hope scatter in the wind

Making it to the birthing grounds of dreams

the stomping grounds

/

To dig up your roots and eat them, to crawl and bury myself in your soul

Isn’t a bad wish

Nor soaring in the still sky, singing like the morning dove

Or being as dandy as a lion, smoldering into oblivion

/

Blow a wish and tell me

And I'll draw dots on our fingers

And tell you mine

Just to wash our hands

Should've grabbed a different plant, thyme

/

So when my spine converges with the earth

And my mind seeps into soil

I will take the dandelion and make a wish

Without ever telling a soul
